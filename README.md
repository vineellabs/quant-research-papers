# Research papers and projects — Vineel Yellapantula

## IAQF Academic Affiliate Student Competition 2020 — winning paper

**He Wins or She Wins, You Win** (`iaqf-2020/He_Wins_or_She_Wins_You_Win.pdf`)

Team *Quants by Ganges*, Cornell University MEng in Financial Engineering:
Nikunj Agarwal (captain), Subham Behera, Harsh Parsuram Puria, Vineel Yellapantula.
Advisor: Sasha Stoikov, Cornell Financial Engineering Manhattan.

The 2020 problem asked teams to analyse two election-linked equity portfolios issued as
structured notes by a Swiss bank and to decide whether, and how, an investor should bet on the
outcome of the 2020 US presidential election. Our paper runs an event study over nine
elections (1984–2016), ties portfolio residual returns to Iowa Electronic Markets betting
probabilities, tests political-donation and factor-exposure explanations, and then rebuilds the
two portfolios with a supervised-learning stock-selection model and backtests the result.

Selected as one of five winning teams out of 36 submissions from 20 academic programs.
Submissions were anonymous, so the paper itself carries no author names; attribution is on the
